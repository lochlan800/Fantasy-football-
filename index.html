📋Core Strategy — the principles that actually win

FPL is won over 38 gameweeks by compounding small edges, not by one lucky captain pick. These are the fundamentals the top-10k managers live by.

💰 Budget structure Foundation

  • Spend big where points are cheap to predict. Premium forwards & attacking mids score the most consistently — own 2–3 of them.
  • Don't waste money on a premium keeper. A rotating cheap GK pair (~£4.0–4.5m each) frees ~£1.0m for outfield.
  • Target one or two enablers — cheap (£4.0–4.5m) starting defenders/mids who let you afford the premiums.
  • Aim to field 11 nailed starters every week. A playing £4.5m beats a benched £6.5m.

🎯 The template + differentials Balance

  • The "template" = the ~10 players most top managers own. Owning them protects your rank.
  • To climb, you need differentials — but pick 2–3 max. Too many and one bad week tanks you.
  • A good differential is low ownership + good fixtures + on set pieces.
  • Never be differential on the captain unless you're chasing and have nothing to lose.

📅 Fixtures beat form (most of the time) Planning

Rank teams by their next 3–5 fixtures, not last week's result. Buy players entering an easy run before the run starts — you get the price rises and the points.

📈 Price changes & team value Edge

  • Players rise/fall ~£0.1m based on net transfers. Getting early "bandwagon" players builds team value → more spending power later.
  • Never take a hit just to chase a price rise. The 4-point hit rarely pays back.
  • Sell falling players before they drop if you were moving them anyway — don't panic-sell a keeper.

🔁 Transfer discipline Discipline

  • Bank a transfer when there's no clear move — flexibility > activity.
  • Only take a −4 hit if the incoming player projects 4+ more points over the next 2–3 GWs than who leaves.
  • Plan 2–3 GWs ahead so single transfers keep the squad healthy without hits.
  • Avoid knee-jerk moves after one blank — process over panic.
🏆 The one-line philosophy: Own the essential template players, add 2–3 well-chosen differentials, captain the safe high-ceiling pick, plan transfers around fixtures, and save your chips for double/blank gameweeks. Do that consistently and you finish top of the mini-league.

📖Stats Guide — what every stat means & how to use it

Every column on the Stats table explained in plain English — what it is, why it matters, and how to turn it into points.

Golden rule: no single stat picks a player. The trick is combining them — e.g. a player with high xGI90 (creating chances) + low goals (unlucky so far) + easy fixtures + high value is a brilliant buy before everyone else catches on.

🏆 Scoring & form

PtsTotal FPL points this season. The bottom line — but backward-looking. A great player at a new club may have low points but be about to explode.
PPGPoints per game. Fairer than total points because it ignores games missed. Use it to compare a player who's been injured vs an ever-present.
FormAverage points over the last ~30 days. Use it to spot who's hot right now — but don't overpay for a short hot streak.
MinTotal minutes played. The clearest "does he actually play?" signal. Use it to avoid rotation risks — high minutes = nailed on.

⚽ Attacking output & underlying stats

G / AGoals and assists. A goal is worth 6pts for a defender/keeper, 5 for a midfielder, 4 for a forward; an assist is 3pts. The raw attacking returns.
xGI90Expected goals + assists per 90 minutes — the chances he creates/gets, per game. The most predictive stat. High xGI90 but few goals = due a haul, buy now. Low xGI90 but lots of goals = riding luck, sell before the crash.
ICTInfluence, Creativity & Threat index — FPL's own all-in-one attacking score. Use it as a quick "how involved is he?" number. High ICT = heavily involved in his team's attacks.

🛡 Defensive stats

CSClean sheets. Defenders/keepers get 4pts for a clean sheet. Use it (with the clean-sheet % in the picker) to find defenders from mean defences.
SvSaves (goalkeepers). Keepers get 1pt per 3 saves. Use it — a busy keeper at a weaker team can still rack up save points even without clean sheets.

🎖 Bonus points

BPSBonus Points System — a behind-the-scenes score for everything a player does in a match (passes, tackles, saves, etc.). The top 3 BPS scorers in each game get bonus points.
BnsActual bonus points earned (1–3 extra per game). Use it — players who consistently earn bonus quietly add 30–60 points a season. A big hidden edge.

💷 Value, ownership & projections

ValValue = points per £m. Use it to find cheap "enablers" — players who score well for their price, freeing up cash for your premiums.
Own%How many managers own him. High = a "template" player; owning him protects your rank. Low (<10%) = a differential to climb with. Both have their place.
xPtsProjected points next gameweek — our forecast from form, fixtures, availability and xG. Use it to compare transfer targets and pick your captain (sort by xPts, look at the top).

🎯 Quick recipes — how to actually use the Stats table

  • Find a captain: sort by xPts — the top few are your best armband options this week.
  • Find a bargain: filter a position, sort by Val — the best points-per-£m for cheap enablers.
  • Find the best defenders: filter DEF, sort by CS (clean sheets) or Pts.
  • Spot a player about to explode: sort by xGI90, then look for ones with low goals — they're creating chances but haven't cashed in yet.
  • Find bonus-point magnets: sort by Bns — consistent bonus is free points most managers ignore.
  • Beat the crowd: sort by form or xPts, then check Own% — a strong player with low ownership is how you climb your mini-league.

🎴Chip Planner

You get each chip twice per season (one in each half). Chips are worth 15–35+ points when timed well — and almost nothing when wasted. Here's the playbook, plus a tracker for your own plan.

🃏 The four chips What & when

  • Wildcard — free unlimited transfers. Use to fix a broken squad or pivot to a fixture swing. First one: often GW7–9 once the season settles. Second: set up for the DGW run-in.
  • Bench Boost — bench points count. Play it on a Double Gameweek when all 15 have two games. Wildcard the week before to load it.
  • Triple Captain — captain scores 3× instead of 2×. Save for a premium player with a home Double Gameweek against weak sides.
  • Free Hit — one-week unlimited squad, reverts after. Perfect for a Blank Gameweek or to attack a big DGW without wrecking your team.

⏱ Golden rules Timing

  • Don't panic-play chips early. The biggest returns come in the back third of the season (DGW/BGW cluster).
  • Bench Boost + Triple Captain want Double Gameweeks — wait for the fixture list to reveal them.
  • Free Hit is your Blank Gameweek saviour — don't burn it on a whim.
  • An early Wildcard is fine if your squad is genuinely broken by injuries/fixtures — a healthy squad doesn't need one.

©️ How to pick the captain Weekly

  • Best fixture × best player. Premium attacker at home to a weak defence is the default.
  • Favour nailed penalty takers — an extra route to points.
  • Check the ownership: captaining a highly-owned player protects rank; a differential captain is high-risk, high-reward.
  • Double Gameweek player > single-game player almost every time.
  • Set a sensible vice-captain who also plays — if your captain is benched/injured, the VC steps in.

🔁 Transfer decision test Before you click

  • Is the player injured, suspended, or fixture-dead? → move.
  • Will the incoming player out-score the outgoing by 4+ over 3 GWs? → a hit may be worth it.
  • No clear upgrade? → bank the transfer.
  • Chasing a price rise only? → don't.

🧠Winning Edge — the advanced tactics

These are the things that separate the top managers from the pack. Several are now baked into the Auto-build optimiser too.

📊 Underlying stats beat past points Predictive

  • Expected goals & assists (xG / xA): a player with high xG but few goals is due a haul — buy before the crowd. High returns on low xG = riding luck, sell before the crash.
  • Minutes / "nailed": a great player subbed at 60 mins is worth less than a solid one who plays 90 every week. Consistency wins leagues.
  • The Auto-build now reads xG/xA and minutes, so it favours players genuinely due to score and nailed to start.

🎯 Set-piece & penalty takers Free edge

  • A first-choice penalty taker has a bonus route to points every single week — hugely underrated.
  • Corner & free-kick takers pick up extra assists and goal threat.
  • Always know who takes them for your players. The optimiser now gives these takers a boost automatically.

🛡 The new defensive-contribution points 2025/26 rule

A big rule change many casual managers ignore: players now earn bonus points for defensive work — defenders for tackles, interceptions, blocks & clearances; midfielders & forwards for tackles & ball recoveries. This makes hard-working full-backs and defensive midfielders quietly brilliant value — they score even when their team doesn't. The Auto-build factors this in when picking your cheaper enablers.

📈 Ownership & rank tactics Positioning

  • Effective Ownership (EO) = who owns a player plus who captains him. High-EO players are "the crowd" — not owning them is a bet against everyone.
  • Leading your league? Own the popular players to defend your lead.
  • Chasing? You need differentials the leaders don't have to catch up — take more risks.
  • Captain on EO too: the popular armband protects rank; a differential captain is how you leapfrog people.

🥊 Beat your mates specifically Mini-league

  • Cover your rival: if the person you're battling owns a big captain pick, own him too — then they can't gain on you with it. You block them.
  • Know your league type: Classic (total points) rewards steady consistency; Head-to-Head (weekly duels) rewards bolder weekly differentials.
  • Late in the season, watch what your closest rivals are doing and react — sometimes matching them is smarter than chasing points.
